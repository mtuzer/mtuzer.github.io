PixLux 2.0 Privacy

Updated 12 October 2026. This policy describes the rebuilt PixLux 2.0 app and its optional subscription and rewarded-ad features.

Media and projects

Imported videos, photos, voice recordings and projects are processed locally on your device. PixLux does not upload this media to the developer’s servers or provide it to the advertising SDK. Files retrieved from iCloud and destinations you choose in the system Share sheet use those services’ own privacy rules.

Purchases

Apple handles subscriptions and billing. PixLux checks signed StoreKit transactions to determine Pro access. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Saved projects remain editable and standard export remains available when a subscription expires. Optional new Pro actions such as 4K export require active access.

Optional rewarded ads

In the rebuilt app an ad request is made only after you choose to watch a rewarded ad. Google’s Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform SDKs may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertising. PixLux disables publisher ad personalization, publisher first-party ID and the Mobile Ads SDK crash handler. Third-party SDK declarations and policies describe their data processing.

Regional ad consent and Apple’s tracking permission are separate. Declining tracking does not remove free editing or standard export. Required regional privacy options are reachable from PixLux’s Pro settings; tracking permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

Storage and deletion

Projects and pending export rewards are stored locally. Deleting a project removes its app-owned files; uninstalling removes the app’s local data. Files saved to Photos, project backups and copies shared elsewhere are managed separately in those destinations. Account billing and subscription cancellation are managed by Apple.
